Photosensitive seizure warning

A small number of people can have a seizure when they see flashing lights, bright bursts or certain patterns, and this game has them: spell blasts, explosions, potion flashes and flickering torches. It can happen even to someone with no history of seizures or epilepsy. If you or anyone in your family has had seizures, talk to a doctor before playing. Stop playing right away if you feel dizzy, see blurred or changed vision, notice eye or face twitching, jerking or shaking of the arms or legs, confusion or a moment of lost awareness. Parents, please watch children while they play.

Viewer discretion

Fantasy combat and creatures that may frighten younger players: ghosts, demons, ogres, snake trolls and Death with his scythe. Dark dungeons and sudden loud sounds.

VR and play safety

Clear your play area and stay aware of walls, furniture, people and pets. Take a break at least every 30 minutes, and stop if you feel sick, dizzy, tired or have eye strain. Follow your headset maker’s age and safety guidance for children.

Playing online

You only play with people who have your room code. Don’t share personal information with players you don’t know.

Super Gauntlet VR

The classic Gauntlet quest for 1 to 4 heroes, rebuilt in first person. Fight through torchlit dungeons packed wall to wall with ghosts, grunts, demons, lobbers, sorcerers and Death himself. Smash generators, grab food, keys, potions and charms, break open chests, doors and cracked walls, and raid the treasure rooms between dungeons. Play with friends or computer allies. Don't shoot the food.
